The MIT license covers copyright, not identity. The product name (currently "Token Industries",
set in `src/brand.ts` and `electron/brand.cjs`), the brand mark (`src/ui/brand-mark.ts`, the app
icons in `build/icons`) and the robot mascot as the face of the product identify the official
project. If you publish a fork or a modified build:

- Give it your own name, and change both brand files so the window title, installers and
  executables carry it.
- Replace the brand mark and app icons with your own.
- Do not present a robot from this project as the mascot or logo of your fork (for example in
  your icon, store listing or announcements). Using the robots inside the app, as the app does,
  is fine.
- Say that your project is based on this one if you like, but do not suggest it is the official
  version or endorsed by its contributors.

Unmodified builds of an official release may be redistributed under the original name.

Names of other products shown in the app (T3 Code, Claude, Codex, GitHub and others) are
trademarks of their owners and are used only to identify the real tools the robots use.
